01 By the numbers

A bluff is a steep shoreline slope of loose sediment, at least 3 feet high, just above high tide.

77miles highly unstable

  • Build coastal resilience

02 Bluffs 101

Erosion is natural. It becomes a hazard when something we value sits at the edge.

  1. Slope. Steeper faces fail more easily.
  2. Shape. Height and profile change how a bluff responds.
  3. Vegetation. Roots hold sediment. Bare faces erode faster.
  4. Shoreline. Ledge, marsh or beach at the base absorb waves.
  5. Water. Runoff and groundwater weaken bluffs from above and within.
  6. Sea-level rise. Higher water undercuts the toe more often.
  7. No single cause. It's how these factors combine. That's what the rating measures.
